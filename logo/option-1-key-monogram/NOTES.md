# Option 1: Key Monogram

- **Concept:** The W is the C-D-E group of a keyboard. Three white keys form the letter, and the C#/D# pair of black keys are the W's two top counters, with rounded key ends. The thin gaps between the white keys run down to the W's feet. On the black tile the black keys are negative space, so the letter and the keyboard are one shape.
- **Mark:** Rounded square tile (100-unit grid, r=20) with the W in white. Key tops are at y=19 and feet at y=85. The dark lockup leaves out the tile, because the dark background takes the place of the black keys.
- **Wordmark:** "Warnick" in Libre Baskerville Bold (cap height matches the key tops). "PIANO ACADEMY" is in Libre Baskerville Regular, tracked +300 to match the width of "Warnick", with its baseline on the W's feet. Both are converted to outlines. The font is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Colors:** #000 / #fff only. Secondary line is #333 on light and #b0b0b0 on dark. No accent.
- **Small sizes:** The key gaps blur to gray at 16px but the W still reads. Below about 40px lockup height, use the mark on its own.
